# v1.1.17

## What changed

`upgrade` now records a multi-line Manual steps bullet whole. Before this, it
kept only a bullet's first line, so walking `migrations/v1.1.9.md` left two
`stepsOwed` entries cut off mid-sentence. Re-running `upgrade` does not repair
them, and a re-walk of v1.1.9 adds the full texts beside the cut ones rather
than replacing them.
