# v1.3.15

## What changed

The code-standards base your reviews read has changed (#1676): §N6 now says
a bare `fs.writeSync` is not a safe pipe writer. Its new house
clause names `writeFd` from `scripts/lib/write-fd.js` as the writer, and a
bare `fs.writeSync` to fd 1 or 2 stays only where a comment says why.

`scripts/lib/write-fd.js` joins the helper surface, exporting `writeFd`, so
your programs can import it through `resolveCore()`.

The daily repo-checks run now leaves settled findings off `Repo checks:
standing findings` and its session-start notice (#1700): `WAIVED` and
`NO-CRITERIA` from the criteria check, accepted stranded research, and
reviewed clobber SUSPECTs. `npm run status` and a hand run of each check
still list them.

Your session rules gain `## Questions to <founder>` (#1701): only a house
decision reaches the founder, a question being researched first and a
choice research settles acted on and reported with its source; and a value
called house is unsourced until traced. The vendored `grilling`,
`wayfinder` and `to-spec` skills are narrowed to match; `to-spec`'s template gains a
`## Values` section.

The priced-options guard now refuses, once, a `❓` round its close-rivals
check does not judge, where it is sent after fewer than 10 tool calls since
the founder's last message, printing `rule:house-decision` (#1710). Any
tool call since the refusal clears it; a bare re-send is refused again.
It is capped at 3 a session.

`resolve-ticket` refuses a resolution comment with no `**Values:**` line
(#1707), before anything is posted, printing the line's form: each value the
resolution relies on as `<value> — house: #N` or `<value> — untraced`, or
`none`. A not-planned close owes none.

A clobber SUSPECT you have judged a deliberate rewrite goes in your repo's
own `docs/clobber-reviewed.json`, keyed by its identity, `"#N\t<edit time>"`,
to the reason; it then reports as `SUSPECT-REVIEWED`. A closed research
ticket that produced no note goes in your
`docs/stranded-research-allowlist.json` as
`{"artefact": "none", "reason": "..."}`; its `NO-CITATION` then reports as
`NO-CITATION, ACCEPTED`. A file that does not parse, or an entry of neither
shape, is BROKEN.

      through `writeFd`, imported through `resolveCore()`, or comment at the
