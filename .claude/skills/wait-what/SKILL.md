Wait — I don't understand where you've got to. Re-pitch it.

- First, do you need me at all? If you can do it or find it out yourself, do that and tell me the result.
- Keep it short: plain words, short sentences, no jargon.
- No references I would have to go and read — no bare issue numbers, file paths or rule names. Say what the thing is instead.
- Say why I care: what changes for me or the business.
- If I asked where we are: what is done, what is left, and what, if anything, waits on me.
- If you need me to decide, restate it from scratch: the question, my options, why I would pick each, and your confidence in each.
- Hold this register for the rest of the session.
