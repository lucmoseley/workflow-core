A RECURRING HAND-OVER IS FIXED, NOT REPEATED (#5786). Before handing the founder an act, search the closed issues for the same act — a deploy, a migration, a grant, a paste: `gh search issues --state closed '<the act>'`. One earlier occurrence makes it recurring: then the recommended option is the durable fix, one that removes the hand-over for later sessions, such as a script and one narrow permission rule. Doing it by hand again stays an option, never the recommended one.

A round carrying `Blocked:` reports the search on a line of its own: `Recurrence: none — <the search you ran>`, or `Recurrence: #<n>, #<n> — (<letter>) repeats it`, naming the earlier issues and the option that hands the act over again. **Enforced** on the round: `scripts/guard-priced-options.js` refuses one with no `Recurrence:` line, one the session ran no closed-issue search for, one naming an earlier issue but no repeating option, and one recommending the option it names. **Unenforced** on a hand-over with no round.
