THAT CALL WAS KILLED BY A SIGNAL — exit 137 is SIGKILL, 143 is SIGTERM — NOT
STOPPED BY AN ERROR OF ITS OWN. DO NOT DEBUG IT AS YOUR OWN BUG YET.

A build, test or session dying with no error of its own is probably earlyoom,
the machine's out-of-memory killer (`docs/environment.md` §3). Run `memcheck`
first.

Not certainly earlyoom, and not certainly a backgrounded job either: the
harness's bg-shell pressure reaper SIGTERMs those (`docs/environment.md` §5),
and a `kill` or `timeout -s KILL` in your own command exits the same way.
