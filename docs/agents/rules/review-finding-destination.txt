EVERY REVIEW FINDING NEEDS A DESTINATION. Before this session ends, each is exactly one of: FIXED (name the commit), TICKETED (filed NOW under the reviewed issue's parent, cited by number; prose on a closed issue never resurfaces) or DECLINED (one line why). A fix ticket, one filed from a whole-spec review, files under the spec only a live fault: a route ordinary use reaches and its wrong behaviour. Its corner or tidying goes standalone, citing the spec. Ticket what you would fix given time, named smell or not. The resolution comment's `**Outstanding:**` line names the tickets or says none, or the close is denied.
