EVERY REVIEW FINDING NEEDS A DESTINATION. Each is FIXED (name the commit), TICKETED (filed NOW under the reviewed issue's parent) or DECLINED (say why; only what you would not fix given time). FIX FIRST any finding in code the reviewed work wrote unless another session claims its file or it needs its own design; ticket those and what the change only exposed. A whole-spec review's TICKETED line names its exception: `claimed by <session name>`, `needs its own design: <reason>` or `exposed, not caused`, or is refused (**Enforced**: the tag, not its truth). A whole-spec review's fix ticket files under the spec only a live fault; the rest go standalone, citing the spec. A no-behaviour fix gets a Standards-only re-review over its diff (`--no-spec`): FIXED or DECLINED. The `**Outstanding:**` line names the tickets or says none, or the close is denied.
