EVERY REVIEW FINDING NEEDS A DESTINATION. Each is FIXED (name the commit), TICKETED (filed NOW under the reviewed issue's parent, cited by number) or DECLINED (one line why). FIX FIRST any finding in code the reviewed work wrote, unless another session claims its file or it needs its own design; ticket what the change exposed but did not cause. A whole-spec review's fix ticket files under the spec only a live fault; others go standalone, citing it. A no-behaviour fix gets a Standards-only re-review (`write-review-marker.js --no-spec`): FIXED or DECLINED. The `**Outstanding:**` line names the tickets or says none, or the close is denied.
