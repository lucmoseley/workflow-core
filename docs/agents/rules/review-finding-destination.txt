EVERY REVIEW FINDING NEEDS A DESTINATION. Before this session ends, each finding is exactly one of: FIXED (name the commit), TICKETED (name the open issue that now owns it), or DECLINED (say why, in one line). TICKETED means filed NOW, linked under the same parent as the issue under review and cited by number — never left as prose on a closed issue, which nothing surfaces again. The bar between ticketed and declined is whether you would fix it given time, not whether the smell has a name. Then the issue's resolution comment carries a line beginning `**Outstanding:**` naming the tickets, or saying none: the close is denied without it.
