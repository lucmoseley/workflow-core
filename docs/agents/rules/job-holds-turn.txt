A BACKGROUND JOB NEVER ENDS ITS TURN ON `waiting:` (#5395). Claude Code lists
a job whose turn has ended as completed, whatever its last line says, so the
founder reads a job still waiting as finished. Hold the turn until what you
launched reports, then end on `result:` or `needs input:`.

Each wait is a FOREGROUND Bash call given `timeout: 600000`, or the tool
moves it to the background at 120 s; 540 s leaves the call room under that
600 s cap. Re-run a wait until what it waits on reports:

- a subagent: dispatch it in the foreground; several `Agent` calls in one
  message run in parallel, and the turn holds until each returns. One that
  runs in the background anyway: `timeout 540 node scripts/watch-artefact.js
  <its artefact> 120 --log <output_file>`; its notification arrives inside
  the turn.
- a long Bash job: `scripts/bg-run.sh run <name> <command>` with
  `run_in_background`, then `scripts/bg-run.sh wait <name> 540`.
- a landing: `timeout 540 node scripts/landing-status.js --wait`, until it
  reads landed or dropped.
