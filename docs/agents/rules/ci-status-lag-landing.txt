A PUSH TO YOUR QUEUE REF IS TESTED BY THE QUEUE LANDER, NOT ON YOUR SHA ALONE.

The queue lander merges the waiting queue refs onto `main` as one batch,
`lander/batch`, runs the full suite there, and moves `main` only to a green
batch. Run `node scripts/landing-status.js` ONCE after every push, docs-only
included, and read it: each of your commits reads `landed:` or `queued:`.

Where a scheduled sweep runs, `npm run check:ci` reads it, and it tests `main`:
its verdict covers what has landed, never what you just pushed.
