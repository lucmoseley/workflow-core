A PUSH TO YOUR QUEUE REF IS TESTED BY THE QUEUE LANDER, NOT ON YOUR SHA ALONE.

The queue lander merges the waiting queue refs as one batch onto `main`, or
onto a batch still under test, pushes it to a `lander/batch-<k>` ref, runs the
full suite there, and moves `main` only to a green batch, in queue order. Run `node scripts/landing-status.js --wait` in the background after every
push, docs-only included, and read it when it exits: each of your commits reads
`landed:` or `queued:`, and its `wait:` line says landed, dropped or bound.

Where a scheduled sweep runs, `npm run check:ci` reads it, and it tests `main`:
its verdict covers what has landed, never what you just pushed.
