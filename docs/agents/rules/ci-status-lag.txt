THIS PUSH DOES NOT TRIGGER A SUITE RUN, AND NOTHING WILL REPORT ON YOUR SHA.

The whole suite runs on the scheduled sweep alone (mantahostels#910), four a day
(mantahostels#3490), so the local checks are the session's only verdict
for up to ~12 hours. Run `npm run check:ci` ONCE after every push, docs-only
included, and read it; never rerun it. It and `check-ci-status.js --notify`
answer off the newest sweep and the COMMIT RANGE it covered, never the sha you
just pushed: nothing new until the next sweep lands.

THE RANGE IS WHAT IT COSTS. At ~9.4 commits/hour a 6-hourly sweep covers ~56
commits, so localising a red by bisection is ~6 full-suite runs and not one.
THE CRON IS NOT WHAT SETS IT: GitHub delivers slots hours late and drops some
outright, and the widest measured gap between DELIVERED sweeps is 11h54m — ~112
commits, ~7 runs (mantahostels#2204). It is a verdict read AFTER THE FACT over a range —
never a precondition of a push, and no session waits on one (mantahostels#2102).
