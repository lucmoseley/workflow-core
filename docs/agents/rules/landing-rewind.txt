NEVER FORCE-PUSH OR DELETE `landing` OR A QUEUE REF, FROM ANY TREE.

`landing` stays on the remote as the cutover's rollback, and a queue ref holds
work the queue lander has yet to land. A force-push (`--force`,
`--force-with-lease`, a `+` refspec) or a deletion drops the commits the ref
holds, and the lander's batch with them.

UNDO A COMMIT WITH A FOLLOW-UP COMMIT — `git revert <sha>`, or the fix itself —
pushed to your own queue ref as any other.
