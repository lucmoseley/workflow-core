NEVER FORCE-PUSH OR DELETE `landing`, FROM ANY TREE.

`landing` is the one branch every session pushes to, and the lander tests and
reverts on it. A force-push (`--force`, `--force-with-lease`, a `+` refspec)
or a deletion drops every commit a neighbour pushed after the one you name, and
the lander's batch with them.

UNDO A COMMIT WITH A FOLLOW-UP COMMIT — `git revert <sha>`, or the fix itself —
pushed to `landing` as any other.
