**A weakened check is never the fix for a blocked run, and a commit that weakens one names it on a `Weakens:` line.** A commit is refused, until its message carries a `Weakens:` line naming each one, where its staged diff turns a module off in `.claude/workflow-core.json` (a `disabled` entry added, or a `modules` entry dropped), removes a `check-*`, `guard-*`, `check:` or test command from a workflow under `.github/workflows/`, or adds a skip (`.skip`, `skipIf`, `xfail`, `mark.skip` and their kin) to a test file that exists at HEAD, or where it runs with a gate's `WFC_<NAME>_OFF` stand-down set. Write one line per weakening, naming what the refusal names, e.g. `Weakens: check-snapshot-restatements.js — dropped from ci.yml until #123 fixes its false positives`. Where the commit also carries unrelated work, split the weakening into a commit of its own, with its line (#2235).
