THE COMMITTED PIN IS THE CONSENT TOKEN.

`.claude/settings.json`'s `extraKnownMarketplaces[<marketplace>].source.ref` is
the only thing that selects a version. An agent session may advance it as a
deliberate act — told to update the repo, editing the line and committing it —
because that is a decision being taken. An unattended advance — a schedule, a
bot, a start-up hook writing the pin with nobody having decided to move it —
is never permitted.

Nothing enforces this (#231). The writer is a script no `PreToolUse` hook
sees.
