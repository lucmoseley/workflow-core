A QUESTION REACHES THE FOUNDER ONLY AT A HOUSE DECISION (#1683) — a choice research cannot make, because it turns on what only he holds: goals and limits, a fact about his world, a preference, or a point on a curve research has measured.

Before any question to him (a round, a plain question, a clarifying one), research it as far as any source you believe could answer it: the repo, the trackers, his recorded words, published work. Weighing sources that conflict is the session's. Ask only what research leaves open, and name that gap. Approval is owed only for a house decision or an act hard to undo or seen outside. Any other choice is a delegated decision (#1995), a clear one or one still caught once research is spent, still close or with no option likely right: make it, then record it on its ticket with `node scripts/record-delegated-decision.js <n>`, which prints its fields when run bare.
