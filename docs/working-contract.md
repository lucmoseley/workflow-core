# Working contract

How work is sequenced and approved on this project.

## Working patterns

<!-- guard-registry:begin -->
- **Only a house decision reaches the founder** (`GLOSSARY.md`). Before any question, a clarifying one included, research it as far as any source can: the repo, trackers, his recorded words, published work. Ask only the gap left, named. Approval is owed only for a house decision or an act hard to undo or seen outside; any other choice is a delegated decision: make it, then run `node scripts/record-delegated-decision.js <n>`. **Enforced** on a close round, and on any other sent under 10 tool calls after his last message: `scripts/guard-priced-options.js` refuses it, printing `rule:house-decision`.
- **Nothing is reported or requested to Anthropic, of any kind** (#1967). A harness gap gets an in-repo fix or workaround, or is recorded as a harness limit. **Enforced** on the `gh` route: `scripts/guard-dispatch.js` refuses a `gh` issue, PR or API write it ties to an `anthropics/*` repo, printing `rule:anthropic-report`. Unguarded: a GraphQL target named by node id or a file-read query, an unreadable repo, a script, `curl` or alias, and a Chrome form.
<!-- guard-registry:end -->
- **A value called house is unsourced until traced** to a source or to a ruling that made a house decision; his rulings mostly took an agent's advice, which is no source. Failing both, research it, and report where it departs from what research calls optimal. A ruling binds until re-ruled. A source is published research or a standard, a vendor's docs or code, or a measurement you can rerun; an agent's recommendation, or a document or ruling resting on one, is not.
- **Build for the repo's long-term health, not the task's quickest finish.** Where a durable fix and a quick one both finish the task, take the durable one even when it costs more now: fix the cause, not the symptom, and leave the code in the shape the next change needs, with no workaround, second copy of a path or exception row for a later session to clear. The extra effort is never on its own a reason for the quick one. Durable means better for the changes the repo will really make, never a layer, option or abstraction with no caller today. A fix too big for the session becomes its own ticket; a stopgap that must land first, such as for a red `main` that blocks everyone, says so in its commit and links that ticket. **Unenforceable**: no hook tells a quick fix from a durable one.
- Queue actionable items at the moment they're agreed — including recommendations buried in prose, not just explicit requests. Audit against that compiled ledger.
- **Test a blocker before it ends a turn** — run the command, attempt the edit, check the tool — rather than inferring it can't be done. Where it genuinely can't be finished this session, record the verified reason where the work lives (a ticket comment or new ticket) and keep going on whatever else is reachable.
- **No paid research or data APIs.** What is banned is buying *facts* — a source, a feed, anything paid to answer a question. A paid tool the repo runs on is not reached by this line at all; it is an ordinary approve-before-execute call. Recommend free keyless sources and free tiers that need no card; say plainly when a tool is crippled without payment. A free tier behind a signup is welcome.
